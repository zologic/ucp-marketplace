Frequently Asked Questions

How does pricing work?

€99/year for the UCPReady plugin + 5% commission on sales generated through our directory. If you make no sales from our platform, you only pay the €99 yearly fee.

Do I need to integrate payments?

No. Customers complete checkout on YOUR WooCommerce store using YOUR payment gateway. We never touch customer payment information.

How do you track sales for commission?

Our plugin adds a referral ID to checkout sessions from our directory. When an order completes, a secure webhook notifies us. We verify the sale with Ed25519 cryptographic signatures.

Can I control which products appear?

Yes. The plugin settings let you exclude specific products or categories from the directory. Only publish what you want to sell through our channel.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime from your merchant dashboard. You'll continue to appear in search results until your annual subscription ends. No long-term contracts.

Do you support multi-currency stores?

Yes. We support EUR, USD, GBP, and other major currencies. Prices display in the customer's local currency if your store supports it.
