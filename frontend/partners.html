Partnership FAQ

What's the white-label pricing model?

Starting at €499/month for the platform + €50/merchant/year for infrastructure. You keep all merchant fees and commissions you charge.

How quickly can I launch?

Most partners are live within 48 hours after domain and branding configuration. Technical integration takes 2-4 hours.

Do you handle merchant onboarding?

You control merchant approval. We provide the UCPReady plugin and technical verification. You set quality standards and pricing.

Can I customize the search experience?

Yes. Full control over UI/UX, search algorithms, merchant ranking, and featured placements. We can also develop custom features.

What support do you provide?

Dedicated Slack channel, priority email support, and quarterly strategy calls. Technical documentation and SDK support included.

Is there an exclusivity requirement?

No exclusivity. You can integrate multiple shopping solutions. We recommend running pilots before full commitment.
