Information We Collect

When you use UCPReady, we collect:

  • Search queries: The products you search for
  • Click data: Products you view
  • Device information: Browser type, IP address, device identifiers

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve search results
  • Analyze platform performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We share minimal information with:

  • Merchants: When you click through to their stores (anonymized)
  • Payment processors: When you complete a purchase
  • Service providers: Infrastructure and analytics partners

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Lodge a complaint with supervisory authorities

Data Retention

We retain search and click data for 90 days for analytics purposes. Order data is retained for 7 years for accounting and legal compliance.
