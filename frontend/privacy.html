Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

Introduction

Who we are: UCPReady operates an AI shopping directory connecting consumers with merchants via the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP).

What this policy covers: This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our platform.

Contact: For privacy questions, email us at privacy@ucpready.com

Data We Collect

1. Consumer Data (Minimal Collection)

  • Search queries: Stored as SHA-256 hash, NOT plaintext. We cannot read your actual search terms.
  • Click events: Merchant clicked, timestamp, temporary session ID (5-minute lifetime)
  • No personal information: We do NOT collect your name, email, address, or payment information
  • Sessions: Temporary session IDs prevent duplicate click counting. Expire after 5 minutes.

2. Merchant Data

  • Domain, business name, contact email
  • Product catalog (synced from your WooCommerce store)
  • Order webhooks (order ID, amount, status - for attribution only)
  • Ed25519 public key (for webhook verification)

3. Admin Data

  • Admin user accounts (email, hashed password, login timestamps)
  • Audit logs (admin actions for security purposes)

How We Use Your Data

  • Search intent analysis: Improve product discovery and merchant matching
  • Attribution: Track which merchants led to sales (for billing purposes only)
  • Analytics: Aggregate statistics for marketplace health monitoring
  • Security: Fraud prevention and system security

Data Sharing

  • We do NOT sell your data
  • Merchant attribution: Merchants receive order attribution data (which searches led to sales) but NOT consumer identities
  • Third parties: We use Stripe for payment processing (merchants only). See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law

Data Retention

  • Search events: 90 days (then aggregated/anonymized)
  • Order attribution: 2 years (for billing disputes)
  • Merchant data: Until merchant account closed
  • Admin audit logs: 1 year

Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your data (with limitations for billing records)
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Contact us: privacy@ucpready.com

Cookies

  • Essential cookies only: Session management for admin dashboard
  • No tracking cookies: We do NOT use third-party tracking or advertising cookies on the consumer frontend

Security

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access control: Admin access requires authentication and is logged
  • Regular audits: We conduct regular security audits
