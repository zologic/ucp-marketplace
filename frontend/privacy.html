Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2026

Overview

We operate an AI-powered shopping directory that helps you discover products across verified merchants. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

When you use our service, we collect:

  • Search queries: The products and terms you search for
  • Click data: Products you view and merchants you visit
  • Device information: Browser type, IP address, operating system
  • Voice recordings (optional): If you use voice search, audio is processed by your browser's Web Speech API and not stored by us

How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and improve search results relevance
  • Analyze platform performance and user behavior
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations and requests
  • Generate anonymized analytics for merchants

Information Sharing

We share minimal information with third parties:

  • Merchants: Anonymized referral data when you click through to their stores
  • Service providers: Infrastructure partners (hosting, CDN, analytics) under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights

We never share: Your personal data with advertisers or sell your information to third parties.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under European data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@shopdirectory.eu

Data Retention

We retain data for the following periods:

  • Search queries: 90 days (for analytics and improvement)
  • Click events: 90 days (for analytics)
  • Order referrals: 7 years (for accounting and tax compliance)
  • Account data: Until account deletion or 3 years of inactivity

Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for site functionality (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (anonymized IP) to understand usage patterns

You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. Note that this may affect site functionality.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and authentication for internal systems
  • Encrypted database storage for sensitive data

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). We ensure adequate safeguards through:< /p>

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions for specific countries
  • Privacy Shield frameworks where applicable

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified via email or prominent notice on our site. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@shopdirectory.eu
  • Data Protection Officer: dpo@shopdirectory.eu
